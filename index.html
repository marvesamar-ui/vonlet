03 INTEGRATED SERVICES

Built around
your risk.

No two sites carry the same risk. Combine trained people, practical procedures and the right technology into one coordinated security plan. Vonlet has provided these services to clients across Zimbabwe and Zambia since 2010.

Build your coverage
ONE PARTNER.
EVERY LAYER.
01

Armed & unarmed guarding

Static posts and roving patrols. Armed officers train intensively in-house with the Zimbabwe Republic Police and the Zambia National Police, and are deployed to higher-risk sites such as mines, fuel depots and premises holding valuable goods.

02

K-9 services

Watch and attack dogs supplied with handlers, plus dog handling training. The unit uses motivational training techniques and works alongside guards and law enforcement.

03

Electronic security & CCTV

CCTV systems, access control and intruder alarms — installed, maintained and monitored by Vonlet's technical team, with 24/7/365 support on protection.

04

Rapid response & monitoring

Alarm systems linked to Vonlet's control room, plus a real-time guard tour platform built on NFC, beacon and QR-code tags that reports each checkpoint scan as it happens.

05

Cash-in-transit

Management of the cash collection service: task assignment, route selection and accounting for all books, supported by Vonlet's branded fleet.

06

Undercover guards

Discreet guards placed into your workplace to work alongside your staff, report on weak points and help prevent cash theft, shoplifting and vandalism.

04 WHO WE ARE

Growing stronger
since 2010.

Vonlet Security is a security provision oriented company offering efficient and reliable services across Africa. Since 2010 it has made enormous strides in the security industry and established itself amongst indigenous owned security companies, with branches across Zimbabwe and part of Zambia.

Our mission

To be the most customer-centric establishment and to offer the best quality services. We are also tirelessly committed to maintaining high ethical standards, as well as assisting the government in fighting crime in order to ensure safety to the community.

Our vision

To become a well-recognised, outstanding, preferred and sought-after security provider in the Southern Africa region.

Our core values

  • Professionalism
  • Innovation
  • Transparency
  • Excellent service standards
  • Team work

Core business

Manned guarding and static protection, protective clothing and specialised safety gear, technical surveillance and electronic security, 24/7/365 on-call tactical protection, and a dedicated customer service and client support division.

RECRUITMENT PROCESS

Four steps to deployment.

Every candidate is screened before they wear the uniform. These are the steps Vonlet publishes for its recruitment process.

  1. STEP 01

    Evaluate candidates

    All candidates are evaluated one on one. The best are selected for interview.

  2. STEP 02

    Job interviews

    Interviews are conducted in groups and also further done on an individual basis.

  3. STEP 03

    Selection & training

    The successful candidates are selected and undergo training and contract signing.

  4. STEP 04

    Employed & deployed

    The employed officers are then deployed to various work stations.
