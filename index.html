05 WHO WE ARE

Growing stronger
since 2010.

Vonlet Security is a security provision oriented company offering efficient and reliable services across Africa. Since 2010 it has made enormous strides in the security industry and established itself amongst indigenous owned security companies, with branches across Zimbabwe and part of Zambia.

Our mission

To be the most customer-centric establishment and to offer the best quality services. We are also tirelessly committed to maintaining high ethical standards, as well as assisting the government in fighting crime in order to ensure safety to the community.

Our vision

To become a well-recognised, outstanding, preferred and sought-after security provider in the Southern Africa region.

Our core values

  • Professionalism
  • Innovation
  • Transparency
  • Excellent service standards
  • Team work

Leadership

Vonlet is led by its chief executive officer from head office in Queensdale, Harare, working with the operations, technical and customer service teams that run deployments across Zimbabwe and part of Zambia.

Core business

Manned guarding and static protection, protective clothing and specialised safety gear, technical surveillance and electronic security, 24/7/365 on-call tactical protection, and a dedicated customer service and client support division.

RECRUITMENT PROCESS

Four steps to deployment.

Every candidate is screened before they wear the uniform. These are the steps Vonlet publishes for its recruitment process.

  1. STEP 01

    Evaluate candidates

    All candidates are evaluated one on one. The best are selected for interview.

  2. STEP 02

    Job interviews

    Interviews are conducted in groups and also further done on an individual basis.

  3. STEP 03

    Selection & training

    The successful candidates are selected and undergo training and contract signing.

  4. STEP 04

    Employed & deployed

    The employed officers are then deployed to various work stations.
